# NabungFi prototype assets

- `src/car-model.ts` / `src/CarWorkshop.tsx`: original procedural roadster with exactly 100 deterministic component records. No commercial car, toy brand logo, licensed model, downloaded texture or third-party vehicle asset is used. Studs are details within a component; they do not represent additional savings units.
- `public/audio/building-loop.wav`: edited from **Lego building (classic)** by **MalarBrush / Stefan Basa**, the user-selected [Pixabay recording](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-lego-building-classic-208359/). Retrieved 2026-09-28. The source page lists the [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/) and marks this track as Content ID registered. The original MP3 is retained in `assets/audio/pixabay-building-208359.mp3` (SHA-256 `5368e693f6b31f10798eabbd9aa887f40b59d614b5a50a7281db92306453691f`). Trimmed 0.18–1.88 seconds, DC removed, 18ms overlap at the loop seam, peak normalized to 0.82. Used as integrated assembly feedback, not offered as a standalone download or branded as an original recording. The site listing is not independent verification of third-party rights.
- `public/audio/build-complete.wav`: original synthesized impact from `scripts/prepare-audio.py`; descending low body, midrange knock and brief filtered-noise transient. No game recording is used for this completion cue.
- `src/sound.ts`: Web Audio playback of the two local assets. The building loop begins on the first piece arrival; completion is a separate single cue. A shared gain/compressor, fades and explicit cancellation control overlap and mute.
- The original MP3 is retained locally and excluded from Git. To reproduce both WAVs, obtain the credited recording from its source page, put it at `assets/audio/pixabay-building-208359.mp3`, then run `python scripts/prepare-audio.py` from `apps/web` using Python with NumPy and SoundFile. The script checks the source hash before rendering. The committed WAVs are sufficient to run the app. The user-supplied Myinstants build/construction/breaking links are references only and are not bundled.
- `public/brand/nabungfi-mark.svg`: original code-native three-color N following the user's six-course construction. Left course five and right course two count from the bottom, with two middle rectangular connectors making a continuous blue path. Only column tops retain small studs. `scripts/make-icons.py` derives the stud-free small-use and single-color SVGs, favicon and PWA icons from that master. `src/ui.tsx` uses the small-use mark with a unified Outfit wordmark. No separate geometry or photorealistic exploration image is shipped.
- Outfit and Work Sans: locally served through pinned Fontsource packages. Their upstream SIL Open Font Licenses remain in their package distributions; no proprietary font is used.
- Lucide icons: supplied by the pinned `lucide-react` dependency under its ISC license.
- Solana/Base marks are small contextual chain identifiers, not endorsements. They identify the intended networks while the persistent local-demo notice identifies this build's actual evidence boundary.

No raster generated image, remote product screenshot or stock illustration is part of the shipped UI.

## Additional procedural goal models

The laptop, house and custom 100-component sculpture are original deterministic geometry in `src/goal-models.ts`. They reuse the existing construction animation, optional credited assembly recording and original completion cue; no third-party 3D model or trademarked toy geometry is copied. PWA PNG icons are rasterized from the canonical SVG using Pillow with antialiasing. The maskable mark is inset to remain inside the central safe circle; its warm background may be cropped by the platform.
